2 Strands,
Twisted Together

DNA looks like a ladder that has been twisted. It has 2 side rails and steps in the middle.

  • 1. Sugar + Phosphate: The side wall
  • 2. Base Pairs: The steps in the middle
  • 3. Hydrogen Bond: Holds both strands together
